The World is Dying

A dark corruption spreads across the land, consuming everything in its path. Ancient forests wither, oceans turn black, and once-thriving civilizations crumble to dust. The entity known as OKUBI feeds on chaos — growing stronger with every battle, every betrayal, every fallen warrior.

Only those brave enough to face the corruption head-on can hope to push it back. Rally your allies, purify the tainted zones, and reclaim what was lost. But beware — the deeper you venture, the more OKUBI whispers in your ear.

Lands Beyond Saving

Venture deep enough and the corruption begins to affect your character — warping abilities, distorting vision, and whispering promises of power.

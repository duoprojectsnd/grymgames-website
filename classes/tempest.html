Hybrid DPS

Tempest

The Tempest is a relentless melee fighter who thrives in close-quarters combat. Armed with lightnings and dark energy, Tempests carve through enemies with devastating combos and life-draining abilities.

Role Hybrid DPS
Difficulty Medium
Playstyle Versatile

Origins of the Tempest

Long before the arenas were forged, the Tempests walked between worlds — harvesting the souls of the fallen and binding them to an ancient covenant. They were neither living nor dead, but something in between: sentinels of the threshold, guardians of the passage. When the corruption spread, the Tempests were the first to feel its pull — and the first to weaponize it.

Tempest Concept Art

The Dark Covenant

Every Tempest is bound by a blood pact — a ritual that strips away mortal fears in exchange for an insatiable hunger for combat. Their lightnings are not mere weapons; they are conduits to the void, each strike siphoning life force from enemies and feeding the Tempest's dark power. In battle, they become an unstoppable storm of blades, growing stronger with every soul they claim.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about OKUBI — from gameplay and platforms to community and development.

General

What is OKUBI?

OKUBI is a fast-paced PvP aerial combat game. Players take flight, master unique archetypes, and compete in gladiatorial arenas that seamlessly combine ground and air combat. Think high-speed fights with full vertical freedom — no cooldowns, just pure skill-based aerial warfare.

Who is developing OKUBI?

OKUBI is developed by a solo developer under Grym Games, focused on delivering a high-quality competitive experience with deep lore and innovative combat mechanics.

What engine is OKUBI built on?

OKUBI is built on Unreal Engine 5, leveraging cutting-edge rendering, physics, and networking technology to deliver a visually stunning and responsive combat experience.

Does OKUBI have a story or lore?

Yes. OKUBI features rich lore involving ancient factions, dynamic corruption, and a sentient entity called OKUBI that watches over every battle. The world reacts to player actions — spawning catastrophic events or blessing entire zones with treasure. Explore the full lore on our Universe page.

Release & Platforms

When does OKUBI release?

OKUBI is currently in active development with a live playtest available on Steam. The full release date will be announced once the game exits Early Access. Follow our news for the latest timeline updates.

What platforms will OKUBI be available on?

OKUBI is being developed for PC (Steam) first. Console releases are being considered and may follow after the full PC launch.

Is there crossplay?

Crossplay is being explored for the future. The initial launch focuses on delivering the best possible experience for PC players.

Is OKUBI free to play?

The final pricing model will be confirmed closer to launch. The current playtest is completely free to join on Steam — no purchase required.

Gameplay

What game modes are available?

OKUBI features multiple arena modes:

  • 1v1 — Duel of Fates: Pure skill, no allies.
  • 2v2 — Blood Pact: Duo synergy and coordinated plays.
  • 3v3 — Trial of Three: Team composition and adaptation.
  • 4v4 — Wanted: Bounty hunt with rotating targets.

Both ranked and unranked queues are planned for all modes.

How does flying work in combat?

Players can deploy wings mid-fight with no cooldown and no mount. Combat seamlessly transitions between ground and air — chain combos from above, dodge mid-air, dive-bomb opponents, and chase fleeing enemies across the sky. Ground and air are one battlefield.

What are archetypes?

Archetypes are OKUBI's class system. Each archetype has unique abilities, playstyles, and combat roles — from tanky frontliners absorbing damage to agile aerial assassins striking from above. Team composition and archetype synergy are key to victory.

What is the Corruption system?

Corruption is a dynamic world event system driven by the entity OKUBI itself. When it stirs, entire zones are affected — catastrophic events rain destruction, or players are blessed with rare buff zones, treasure chests, and resource surges. Purification raids and world boss events keep the world alive and unpredictable.

Is there a ranking system?

Yes. OKUBI features a competitive ranked ladder across all arena modes. Climb the ranks, earn exclusive seasonal rewards, and prove your mastery against the best players.

Getting Started

How do I join the playtest?

Visit the OKUBI Steam page and request access to the playtest. Once approved, you can download and play immediately.

What are the system requirements?

Minimum (estimated):

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • CPU: Quad-core processor (Intel i5 / AMD Ryzen 5)
  • RAM: 8 GB
  • GPU: NVIDIA GTX 1060 / AMD RX 580 or equivalent
  • Storage: 20 GB available space
  • DirectX: Version 12

Recommended specs will be updated as development progresses and optimization continues.

Monetization & Fairness

Is OKUBI pay-to-win?

No. OKUBI is committed to fair competition. All purchasable items are cosmetic only and provide zero gameplay advantage. Skill wins matches, not spending.

Will there be cosmetic customization?

Yes. Players can customize their characters with skins, wings, armor sets, and emotes. Cosmetics are earnable through gameplay progression and also available in the in-game shop.

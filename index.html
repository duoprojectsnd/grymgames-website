Take to the Skies

Winged Combat

Deploy your wings mid-fight and take the battle to the air. OKUBI's hybrid combat system lets you seamlessly transition between ground combos and aerial engagements — chain attacks from above, dodge mid-air, dive-bomb opponents, and chase fleeing enemies across the sky.

No flight cooldown. No mount. Just pure vertical freedom integrated directly into combat. Ground and air are one battlefield.

Random events triggered by OKUBI that can bless or curse an entire zone. Rare loot surges, blood moons, void rifts, safe havens — no warning, no pattern. The world shifts in an instant. Adapt or die.

Corruption grows across zones over time, warping the land and empowering hostile creatures. If players don't push it back, OKUBI itself manifests — and wipes everything in its path. The balance of the world rests in every player's hands.

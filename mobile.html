Questions

FAQ

General
What is OKUBI?▼
OKUBI is a fast-paced PvP aerial combat game. Players take flight, master unique archetypes, and compete in gladiatorial arenas that seamlessly combine ground and air combat. Think high-speed fights with full vertical freedom.
Who is developing OKUBI?▼
OKUBI is developed by a solo developer under Grym Games, focused on delivering a high-quality competitive experience with deep lore and innovative combat mechanics.
What engine is OKUBI built on?▼
Unreal Engine 5, leveraging cutting-edge rendering, physics, and networking technology.
Does OKUBI have lore?▼
Yes. Rich lore involving ancient factions, dynamic corruption, and a sentient entity called OKUBI that watches over every battle. The world reacts to player actions.
Release & Platforms
When does OKUBI release?▼
Currently in active development with a live playtest on Steam. Full release date TBA.
What platforms?▼
PC (Steam) first. Console releases may follow after full PC launch.
Is OKUBI free to play?▼
Final pricing TBA. The current playtest is completely free on Steam.
Gameplay
What game modes?▼
1v1 Duel of Fates, 2v2 Blood Pact, 3v3 Trial of Three, 4v4 Wanted. Ranked and unranked.
How does flying work?▼
Deploy wings mid-fight, no cooldown, no mount. Seamless ground-to-air combat transitions.
What are archetypes?▼
OKUBI’s class system. Each has unique abilities and combat roles — from tanks to aerial assassins.
Is there ranked?▼
Yes. Competitive ranked ladder across all modes with seasonal rewards.
